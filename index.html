      Cook window, 1:00–4:30

      One oven, four burners. Bird 1 sears in the braiser; bird 2 roasts on the half sheet (or in the host's 12" skillet, if there is one). The 10" cast iron is kept for the wagyu.

      Step by step

        After dinner

          Chickens: pull each bird when its breast reads 150°F (ATK) and rest it at least 15 minutes; carryover finishes it. Thighs should read about 170°F (my number; the video doesn't give one). If both pans don't fit on one oven rack, use two racks and swap halfway.
