        After dinner

          Chickens: pull each bird when its breast reads 150°F (ATK) and rest it at least 15 minutes; carryover finishes it. Thighs should read about 170°F (my number; the video doesn't give one). If one end browns faster, turn the pan halfway.

          Clear out first

            Rules the map follows

            • Raw chicken lives lowest (bottom shelf, then the crisper), always on a tray.
            • The wagyu stays sealed in the deli drawer. It's eaten rare, so it never sits under chicken.
            • Desserts and sauces get the top two shelves.
            • Hot food goes in within 2 hours: uncovered until cold, then covered.
            • The freezer is the overflow: ice packs from Sunday, and the cake layers overnight if shelf 3 gets crowded.
